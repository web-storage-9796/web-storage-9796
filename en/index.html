Lock Replacement in Barcelona

Specialists in lock replacement, high-security cylinders and protective escutcheons

When should you change your lock?

After moving or buying a home, if you have lost your keys, after a break-in attempt, or if your cylinder is over 10 years old. We replace your lock with a high-security anti-bumping, anti-pick and anti-drill cylinder with an ownership card, in the same visit whenever possible.

Security escutcheon installation

The protective escutcheon is the most effective defence against cylinder snapping and extraction, the most common method in home burglaries. We install anti-snap and magnetic escutcheons from leading brands, compatible with armoured, reinforced and communal doors.

Emergency door opening

Locked out of your home? In Barcelona city we arrive in 20-30 minutes and open the door without damage whenever possible. We confirm the price before starting — no surprises.
