Lots of 1960s and 70s housing: reinforce before replacing

L'Hospitalet is one of the most densely populated cities in Europe. Much of its housing was built between the 1950s and the 1970s, when neighbourhoods such as la Florida, Pubilla Cases, Can Serra and Bellvitge grew with tall residential blocks. Many of those flats still have their original front door, with a basic lock and a standard cylinder that no longer offers today's protection. Before replacing the door, it usually makes more sense to upgrade what locks it:

  • A security cylinder that resists bumping, picking and drilling, with a sacrificial break line that protects it against snapping.
  • A security shield that covers the cylinder from outside and makes it harder to pull out or drill.
  • An extra deadbolt or a multi-point lock, if the door leaf and frame allow it.
  • A reinforced or armoured door when the original door has reached its limit.

Before you buy a cylinder at a DIY store

Many people buy a cylinder at a large DIY store and fit it themselves. It can be done, but some common mistakes leave the door worse off than before. If you would rather we do it, we measure the door and suggest a compatible model: more on lock change.

  • Wrong size. A euro cylinder is measured from the fixing screw to each end (for example 30 × 40 mm), not by its total length.
  • Cylinder sticking out. If it protrudes more than about 3 mm beyond the door or the shield, it is much easier to snap or pull out.
  • No shield. A good cylinder without a shield is still exposed to snapping and extraction.
  • Key left on the inside. If the key is usually left in the lock at home, an emergency-function (double-clutch) cylinder lets you open from outside in an emergency.

What to tell us when calling from L'Hospitalet

In large blocks, one address can have several stairwells. With these details we reach the right door sooner:

  • Neighbourhood, street and number, and the stairwell or block if there are several.
  • Floor and door, and whether there is a lift.
  • Whether the door was pulled shut or locked with the key, and whether a key is left on the inside.
  • A photo of the lock and cylinder on WhatsApp, if you can take one.
