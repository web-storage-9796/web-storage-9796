Diagnosis before repairing a blind

A blind that does not go up, goes down crookedly or produces noise can have different causes. We review the mechanism and the condition of its elements to determine if a specific part should be adjusted, repaired or replaced. This way we avoid changing components that still work correctly. Repairing the right part avoids unnecessary costs.

Blinds for premises: safety and operation

In a premises, a blind in poor condition can affect access and daily operations. After inspecting the closure, we indicate the necessary intervention and the options available to recover its operation safely so that your business can open and close with peace of mind.

Common breakdowns in manual and electric blinds

A worn belt or collector, displaced slats, a damaged shaft or loose guides can prevent movement. In motorized blinds, the power supply, control, motor and limit switches are also checked. The diagnosis allows you to decide between adjusting, changing a part, motorizing or replacing when the assembly no longer compensates.
