Services for your building's shared doors

Shared entrances are used many times a day and need reliable solutions. We work on doors, locks and access systems in residential buildings, assessing their current condition and what residents and property managers need before proposing any work. We aim for durable solutions that stand up to daily use.

  • Fitting and adjusting hydraulic door closers and hold-open devices.
  • Cylinders, handles, knobs, locks and broken key extraction.
  • Keyed-alike and master key systems.
  • Electric door releases, video intercoms and other access systems.

One key for several doors

Where it makes sense to simplify key management, we design keyed-alike or master key systems. The set-up depends on the existing doors, who needs access and how much control is required. Fewer keys, and each person opens only the doors they should.

Working with property managers and residents' committees

Work in a residential building may need access to common areas, coordination with residents and prior approval of the quote. We define the scope, check the doors and set out a detailed proposal so whoever is responsible can review it before the work is scheduled. Depending on the building, we can also look at entrances, garages, storage rooms, plant rooms and fire doors.

Preventive maintenance for the main entrance

A building's street door opens and closes many times a day. Checking it before it fails stops the building being left with the entrance open, or a neighbour being unable to get in. In a check-up we look at:

  • Whether the door closer shuts the door fully, without slamming and at the right speed.
  • Whether the latch engages properly in the strike plate, so the door doesn't rest closed without locking.
  • Whether the electric door release opens the door from every flat.
  • Wear on the cylinder, hinges or pivots, and on fire doors that must close by themselves.
  • The garage pedestrian door and access to storage rooms and plant rooms.

Lost or stolen entrance keys

When a neighbour loses a key to the street door, the cylinder doesn't always need replacing. If the key ring has no address and the key can't be linked to the building, the community may decide to keep it. If the keys were stolen along with ID, or nobody knows who holds some copies, it's worth changing.

If it is changed, the community decides how many copies it needs and we hand them over counted, ready to give out to residents. In a building with a master key system, the change is planned using the system's registration card, so each resident's key still opens their own door and the shared areas.

Quotes and invoices for the community

We prepare a written quote setting out the scope of the work, so the property manager or the owners' meeting can approve it before it's scheduled. The invoice is issued with VAT in the name of the community of owners, with its tax number, ready for the building's accounts.

Payment is made when the job is finished, by bank transfer, Visa or MasterCard, Bizum or cash. If the problem is urgent, such as a street door that won't close, call or WhatsApp us and we'll tell you when we can come, Monday to Friday, 8 am to 8 pm.
