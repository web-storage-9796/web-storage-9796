An installation suited to the entrance

A door isn't chosen for its looks alone. The size of the opening, how it's used, the lock, cylinder, handles, closing system and the security required are all part of the assessment. Before installing, we check the entrance to suggest a compatible solution. The result has to suit how each door is used.

  • Standard, armoured and high-security doors.
  • Entrances to homes, shops and residential buildings.
  • Assessment of the lock, cylinder and security shield.
  • Check of handles, door closers and other closing hardware.

Anti-squatter steel doors

If your home or premises will be left empty, you can protect the access with a high-strength steel anti-squatter door. It is installed and removed without damaging your entrance door or doing building work. Learn about anti-squatter door installation in Barcelona.

Security comes from the whole door

An entrance door works as a system: leaf, frame, lock, cylinder, shield and hardware. During the assessment we look at all of them together to recommend improvements that work with each other. Upgrading a single part doesn't always make the whole entrance more secure.

From the existing opening to handover

The installation starts with measuring and checking the frame. Then we define the type of door, finish, opening direction and locking hardware. The proposal covers any removal or adaptation needed, fitting, adjustments and a final check. For shared building entrances, we also check compatibility with door closers, electric door releases and access control.
