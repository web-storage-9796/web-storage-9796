Why install a protective shield

A good bowler hat must be accompanied by adequate external protection. Safety shields help make it difficult to break, remove and drill the cylinder. The installation must be done with the correct model and respecting the characteristics of the door so that the assembly works as intended. A good installation protects without compromising closure.

  • Check of lock, cylinder and door thickness.
  • Anti-break shields and magnetic options as needed.
  • Compatibility with reinforced and armored doors.
  • Joint assessment of shield and safety cylinder.

Shield and lock change: when should it be done at the same time

If the cylinder is already old, does not have current protections or is showing wear, it may be more effective to renew both elements during the same intervention. First we evaluate the state of the installation and explain the alternatives available so that invest only in what your door needs.

Standard, armored or magnetic shield

The choice depends on the cylinder, the lock, the thickness and material of the door, the projection of the cylinder and the level of exposure. A magnetic shield adds a movable cover, while other models prioritize the physical strength of the body and attachment. Not all shields are compatible with all doors: we measure and check the set before recommending it.
