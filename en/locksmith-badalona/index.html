Protect an empty flat before it is too late

Squatting is one of the most searched-for concerns in Catalonia: on Google Trends, "okupas Badalona" is among the most frequent searches on the topic and "cerradura antiokupa" (anti-squatter lock) is one of the fastest growing. No measure is foolproof, but protection works in layers and each one adds time and difficulty:

  • Change the cylinder when you receive the keys, to cancel copies you don't control.
  • A high-security cylinder with a security shield, to resist snapping and extraction.
  • A steel anti-squatter door if the flat will be empty for a while: it is fitted without building work and removed without damaging the front door. More on models and sizes in anti-squatter doors.
  • Regular visits, an empty letterbox and, if you have one, an active alarm: a flat that looks lived in attracts less attention.

If the flat is already occupied

In that case a locksmith should not open the door or change the lock on their own initiative, even if you are the owner: entering or changing the lock of an occupied home without following the legal route can backfire on you. The right step is to report it to the Mossos d'Esquadra or the Guàrdia Urbana and follow the procedure they indicate. Once the property is recovered, we open it, change the lock and, if needed, fit an anti-squatter door so it doesn't happen again.

Bought, inherited or rented flats

When a flat changes hands, the old keys still open the door: former tenants, relatives, neighbours or the estate agent may have them. Changing the cylinder is the quickest way to cancel all those copies. If the flat will keep being rented out, a cylinder with an ownership card limits who can make new copies. See lock change.
