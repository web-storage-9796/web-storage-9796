If the key has snapped or the lock fails

A snapped key, a jammed latch or a worn cylinder aren't always fixed the same way. Once the door is open, we check the lock and, if needed, suggest a repair or replacement so the problem doesn't happen again.

How much it costs to open a door in Barcelona

At Cerrajería Merafex, opening a door that has slammed shut costs €70–110, and a door locked with a key €100–180. An armoured door costs €130–250, and a high-security door starts at €200, quoted case by case. Prices include VAT and apply Monday to Friday, 8 am to 8 pm.

Where you fall within each range depends on the door, the cylinder, the shield and the state of the lock: a door that has simply slammed shut is not the same job as a snapped key or a jammed mechanism. With those details we explain the method we plan to use and confirm the price before we start.

If the cylinder needs replacing afterwards, its price is added. You'll find all our guide prices in the Barcelona locksmith price guide.

Door slammed shut or locked with a key

It's the first thing we'll ask. If the door has slammed shut, only the latch is holding it and opening it is usually quick and damage-free. If it's locked with a key or deadbolt, the cylinder is what's blocking it and the job depends on the model: with some security cylinders it has to be replaced after opening.

Armoured and security doors

Armoured and security doors have several locking points and security cylinders, often protected by a shield. Before starting, we check the cylinder and the shield to choose the method that does the least damage, and we tell you whether any part will need changing afterwards.

After opening: does the cylinder need changing?

Not always. If the key was left inside or the door slammed shut, the lock usually still works. It's worth changing if you've lost your keys or had them stolen, if the cylinder has been damaged or if you don't know how many copies exist. More on lock changes.
