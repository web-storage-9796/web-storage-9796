Moving to El Prat: start with the lock

According to Google Trends, renting and buying flats are among the most searched topics about El Prat. Every move leaves copies of keys with previous tenants, owners, agencies or tradespeople. Changing the cylinder when you move in is the simplest way to know who can open your door. If you rent, discuss it with the landlord first; the usual approach is to keep the original cylinder and refit it when the tenancy ends. See lock change.

Holidays: when locksmith searches peak

In Catalonia, searches for "cerrajero" (locksmith) peak in the New Year week and in late July and August, according to Google Trends: the weeks of trips and holidays. If you are going away (from El Prat, the airport is right next door), before you close the door:

  • Leave a spare key with someone you trust who is not travelling with you.
  • Lock the door with the key, not just pulled shut: a latch is far easier to open than a deadbolt.
  • If you lose your keys together with documents showing your address, change the cylinder when you get back.
  • If the key won't go in when you return or you see marks on the lock, don't force the door: it may be a break-in attempt. Call 112 if you suspect someone has got in.
