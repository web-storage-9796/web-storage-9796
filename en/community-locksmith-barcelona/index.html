Services for your community accesses

Community accesses concentrate many daily uses and require reliable solutions. We work on doors, locks and building access systems, assessing the current state and the needs of neighbors and administrators before proposing an intervention. We prioritize durable solutions for everyday use.

  • Installation and regulation of hydraulic springs and retainers.
  • Cylinders, handles, knobs, locks and key extraction.
  • Equalization or training of cylinders.
  • Electric door openers, video intercoms and other access systems.

One key for several accesses

In communities where it is convenient to simplify key management, we study equalization or master keying systems. The configuration depends on the existing doors, the people who need access and the level of control needed. Fewer keys, with access that corresponds to each person.

Coordination with administrators and presidents

A community action may require access to common areas, coordination with neighbors and prior validation of the budget. We collect the scope, review the access and detail the proposal so that the responsible person can evaluate it before scheduling the work. Portals, garages, storage rooms, technical rooms and fire doors can also be studied depending on the installation.
