Lock Changes in Barcelona

Specialists in lock changes, high-security cylinders and protective shields

When should you change a lock?

After a move or home purchase, if you've lost your keys, after a break-in attempt or if your cylinder is over 10 years old. We replace your lock with a high-security anti-bumping, anti-pick and anti-drill lock with an ownership card, in the same visit where possible.

Security shield installation

The protective shield is the most effective defence against cylinder breaking and extraction, the most common method in residential burglaries. We install leading brand anti-break and magnetic shields, compatible with armoured, reinforced and community doors.

Emergency door opening

Locked out? In Barcelona city we arrive in 20-30 minutes and open without breaking whenever possible. We confirm the price before starting, no surprises.
