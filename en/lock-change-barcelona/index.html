What we check before changing a lock

Not every door needs the same system. We check the lock type, the cylinder size, the condition of the door, whether the handles are compatible and any existing external protection. With that, we suggest a replacement that keeps the door closing properly and improves security. We start from your door, not from a one-size-fits-all solution.

  • Cylinders with an ownership card where the model allows it.
  • Compatible with armoured, high-security and standard doors.
  • Security shields against extraction and drilling.
  • For homes, shops and residential buildings.

Security shield and cylinder: protecting them together

The cylinder is the heart of the lock, but an external shield helps protect it against snapping, extraction and drilling. During the visit, we check whether your door can take a compatible shield and what level of protection makes sense for you. The two work best when chosen together.

Change the cylinder or the whole lock?

If the lock works properly and you just want new keys or a better cylinder, replacing the cylinder may be enough. If the mechanism is worn, has been damaged or can't take the upgrade you want, we look at replacing the whole lock. We also check whether it's a mortice, surface-mounted or multipoint lock, to avoid replacing it unnecessarily.

How we change a lock or cylinder

The process is the same whether you change just the cylinder or the whole lock:

  • We measure the cylinder and check the lock type and the condition of the door.
  • We suggest one or more compatible models and confirm the price.
  • We replace the part and check that the door opens and closes smoothly.
  • We hand over the new keys and, if the model includes one, the ownership card for ordering copies.

How much it costs to change a lock or cylinder

At Cerrajería Merafex, replacing a standard lock cylinder costs €90–150, and a mid-range anti-bump security cylinder €140–220. A high-security cylinder with a key registration card costs €200–350. A complete mortise lock costs €150–250, and the multipoint lock on an armoured door €250–400, parts and VAT included.

There's no single price. Within each range it depends on whether a new cylinder is enough or the whole lock has to go, the cylinder's security level (standard, anti-bump, anti-pick or anti-drill), whether a shield is added or a compatible one is already fitted, and the lock type: mortise, surface-mounted or multipoint. We confirm the price for the model you choose before we start.
