Flats and houses with gardens

Flat doors, house doors and garden gates: we change cylinders, repair locks and open doors without damaging them whenever the lock allows it.

Cylinder and key changes

If you've lost your keys, have a new tenant or don't know how many copies exist, we replace the cylinder with a correctly sized security one.

Building entrances and garages

Locks, door closers and electric strikes on building entrances, plus locks on garage pedestrian doors and storage rooms.

An area of houses, apartment buildings and garages

Sant Gervasi and Sarrià mix detached houses with gardens, apartment buildings from different periods and blocks with shared car parks. Each type of access has its weak point: in a house, the gate or the side door; in an apartment building, the entrance used by many neighbours; in a garage, the pedestrian door that gets left open. That's why we ask which door it is before we go, so we bring the right tools and parts.

Houses with gardens: check every entrance

In a house, reinforcing the front door isn't enough if the gate or garage door opens with a basic key. We check the whole set and tell you what's worth changing and what can wait:

  • Cylinder and lock on the front door.
  • Lock on the gate or garden door.
  • Garage pedestrian door and side doors.
  • Key copies: if you don't know how many there are, it's worth changing the cylinder.

Apartment buildings: entrance, locks and shared keys

For building entrances we work with the residents' association president or the property manager: we check the lock, door closer and electric strike, and we can key-match the entrance, garage and storage rooms so each resident uses a single key. More on community locksmith services.

What to tell us when calling from Sant Gervasi or Sarrià

With these details we get to the right entrance and prepare the visit:

  • Street and number and, for a house, whether access is through a gate or the garage.
  • Whether the problem is in the flat, the building entrance, the garage or a shop.
  • Whether there's a concierge and their hours.
  • A photo of the lock and the door edge on WhatsApp, if you can take one.
