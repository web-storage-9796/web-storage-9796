Bars for openings and fences for perimeters

Bars protect windows, doors, terraces and other openings; fences and railings mark out gardens, shared areas, plots and walkways. Depending on use, we look at fixed, opening or folding designs, as well as the material, anti-corrosion finish, fixings and maintenance. If the work affects a façade or a common element, it's worth checking the permits needed beforehand.

Fixed, hinged or folding bars

The right type of bars depends on how the opening is used:

  • Fixed: for windows that aren't used as an exit. The simplest option, with no lock to maintain.
  • Hinged: opens like a door and locks with a key. Useful on ground-floor windows or patios that could serve as a fire exit, and for cleaning the glass.
  • Folding (collapsible): concertinas to one side. Common on shop fronts, terraces and doors used every day.

The lock on the bars matters too

A set of bars or a gate is only as secure as its lock. As locksmiths, we check the lock along with the bars: if it takes a euro-profile cylinder, a security cylinder can be fitted and, if you like, keyed alike with your front door so you only need one key. For locks exposed to rain, we choose one designed for outdoor use.

What to send us for an initial assessment

Over WhatsApp, these details let us give you a first idea before the visit. To browse models, see rejas.merafex.es.

  • Width and height of the opening, and whether the bars go inside or outside.
  • A photo of the window or door and another of the façade.
  • The type of wall, if you know it: brick, concrete or stone.
  • Whether the window opens outwards or has a roller blind, so the bars don't get in the way.
