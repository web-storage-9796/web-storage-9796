How we manage an opening

When contacting, we collect basic data and location. Upon arrival, the technician checks the door, lock and situation to apply the most appropriate technique. No part is replaced without informing you first.

  • Previous assessment of the closure system.
  • Open priority without breaking when feasible.
  • Price confirmed before starting.
  • Cylinder replacement alternatives if the system is damaged.

If the key has broken or the lock fails

A broken key, a blocked latch or a damaged cylinder are not always solved in the same way. After opening, we check the condition of the lock and, if necessary, we propose a repair or replacement to prevent the incident from recurring.

What influences a door opening

A slammed door does not pose the same intervention as a locked door, a broken key, or a locked mechanism. The type of door, the cylinder, the shield and the state of the closure also influence. With this data we can explain the planned option and what factors form the budget before starting.
