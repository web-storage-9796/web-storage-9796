A former town with buildings on a different scale

Gràcia was an independent municipality until it was annexed to Barcelona in 1897, and it keeps its narrow streets, squares and low-rise buildings. Doors and locks from different periods coexist in these buildings, and many ground-floor homes open directly onto the street. So besides the front door, it is worth checking windows and street-level access points.

Upgrading an old door without replacing it

An old wooden door can still do its job if the leaf and frame are sound. Depending on the case, a modern cylinder, a security shield or a compatible additional lock can be considered. If the wood around the lock is weakened, upgrading the cylinder alone does not make the door more secure, and the support has to be assessed first. More on security shields.

  • Condition of the leaf and frame around the lock.
  • Lock type: mortice or surface-mounted.
  • Whether it takes a standard-size cylinder or needs adapting.
  • Whether a second locking point makes sense.

Ground floors and street-facing windows

Ground-floor windows and those facing interior courtyards are access points worth assessing separately from the door. Bars and closures are chosen according to the opening, the façade and the building's rules. If the window is used for ventilation or as an exit, the solution must open from inside. See security bars and fences and burglary prevention.

During the August Festa Major

In 2025, from 15 to 21 August between 21:00 and 05:00, much of the Vila was closed to traffic, with access for residents and authorised vehicles. If you call us on those days, tell us whether your street is decorated or closed: it helps us plan how to get there. The district publishes the restrictions every year on the Festa Major de Gràcia website (in Spanish).

What to tell us when calling from Gràcia

Gràcia mixes pedestrian streets in the Vila with steep streets in the upper part, near Park Güell. These details help us prepare the visit:

  • Street and number, plus a nearby square or junction as a reference.
  • Whether it is a ground-floor home with direct street access.
  • Whether the street is pedestrian or closed to traffic that day.
  • In la Salut, el Coll or Vallcarca, whether the entrance can be reached by vehicle.
  • A photo of the lock on WhatsApp so we can identify the model.
