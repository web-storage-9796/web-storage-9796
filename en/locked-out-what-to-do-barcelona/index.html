Locked out of your home in Barcelona? What to do

Before you call anyone, run through these steps. There is often a spare key or an insurance policy that covers it, and if you do need a locksmith, get a fixed price first.

Call: 611 37 54 95Send a photo of the door

First, find a spare key

A neighbour, relative, the building's concierge or your letting agent may have one. It is the fastest option and costs nothing.

Slammed or locked?

A door that has slammed shut costs €70–110 to open; a locked one, €100–180. Check before you call.

If anyone is at risk, call 112

If there is a child or a dependent person inside, a fire, or water pouring out, call 112 before a locksmith.

Step by step if you are locked out

Work through them in this order. The first steps are free and often solve the problem.

  • 1. Look for other ways in and spare keys. A neighbour, a relative, the building's concierge or, if you rent, your letting agent or landlord.
  • 2. Check how the door is shut. Slammed shut (latch only) or locked with a key. It makes a big difference: €70–110 versus €100–180 on a standard door.
  • 3. Do not force it. Avoid X-ray film, cards or screwdrivers unless you know what you are doing: you can damage the latch, frame or cylinder and make the job dearer.
  • 4. Check your home insurance. Many policies include emergency locksmith cover. Call your insurer first and ask what is covered.
  • 5. Call a traceable locksmith and get a fixed price including VAT before they come out. Our guide to avoiding locksmith scams lists what to ask.
  • 6. Have your documents ready. ID showing the address, a tenancy agreement or a bill in your name. If everything is inside, tell the locksmith.
  • 7. Afterwards, think about changing the cylinder if the lost keys had anything showing your address.

When to call 112 first

If there is a child, a dependent person or someone not responding inside, or a hob left on or water pouring out, call 112 before a locksmith. The emergency services can get into the property and assess the situation.

If it happens outside our hours

Merafex works Monday to Friday, 8 am to 8 pm, with typical arrival in 20–30 minutes within Barcelona city. We do not work nights or weekends.

If you are locked out outside those hours and it is not urgent (you can stay with someone or wait), waiting for a weekday is usually cheaper than a night call-out. If you cannot wait, ask whoever you call for the total price including VAT before they come. More on our emergency locksmith page.

Lost or stolen keys

If you lost your keys with nothing showing your address, the risk is low. If they were with ID, a letter or an agency key tag, or if they were stolen, it is worth changing the cylinder as soon as possible. If they were stolen, report it to the police as well.

A standard cylinder costs €90–150 to replace, and a high-security cylinder with a key card €200–350; with the card, only you can order copies. See our lock change page.

Who pays for the lock in a rented flat

It depends on the cause and on your contract. Under Spain's Urban Leases Act (LAU, article 21.4), small repairs due to wear from ordinary use are the tenant's responsibility, and repairs needed to keep the property in good condition are the landlord's.

Losing your keys or getting locked out is usually down to the tenant; a lock that breaks from age may be the landlord's job. Check your contract, let your landlord or agent know, and keep the invoice.
