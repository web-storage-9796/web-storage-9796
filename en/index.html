Lock Changes in Barcelona

Lock changes, high-security cylinders and security shields

When should you change a lock?

After a move or home purchase, if you've lost your keys, after a break-in attempt or if your cylinder is over 10 years old. We replace your lock with a high-security anti-bumping, anti-pick and anti-drill lock with an ownership card, in the same visit where possible.

See lock changes

Security shield installation

A security shield covers the outside of the cylinder and makes it harder to snap or pull out, a common technique in home burglaries. We fit anti-snap and magnetic shields from brands such as TESA ASSA ABLOY, CISA, Mul-T-Lock and ISEO, compatible with armoured, high-security and building entrance doors.
