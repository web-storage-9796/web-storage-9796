An installation adapted to access

The door is not chosen only for its appearance. Measurements of the opening, type of use, lock, cylinder, handles, closing system and required security are part of the assessment. Before installing, we review the access to propose a compatible solution. The result must respond to the use of each door.

  • Conventional, armored and armored doors.
  • Access to housing, premises and community.
  • Assessment of lock, cylinder and protective shield.
  • Review of handles, springs and closing elements.

Security from the door assembly

An access door works as a system: leaf, frame, lock, cylinder, shield and hardware. During the assessment, these elements are studied together to recommend improvements that are consistent with each other. Improving a single piece does not always improve all access.

From the existing opening to the delivery of the door

The planned installation begins with measurements and review of the frame. Then the type of door, finish, opening direction and closing elements are defined. The proposal contemplates the necessary removal or adaptation, assembly, adjustments and final verification. In community accesses, compatibility with docks, door releases and access control is also reviewed.
