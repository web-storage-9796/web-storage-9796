How much does a locksmith cost in Barcelona?

A locksmith in Barcelona costs €70–110 to open a door that has slammed shut, €100–180 if it is locked with a key and €90–150 to replace a standard lock cylinder. These are Merafex guide prices, including VAT and parts, Monday to Friday from 8 am to 8 pm.

Below you will find the price range for each job. Send us a photo of the door and lock on WhatsApp and we can give you a closer estimate before we come out.

Door opening prices

What changes the price most is how the door is shut. If only the latch is holding it (slammed shut), it can usually be opened without damaging the lock. If it is locked or it is a reinforced door, the job takes longer and the cylinder sometimes has to be replaced.

Lock cylinder, lock and security shield prices

Replacing just the cylinder is the most common and cheapest option when the lock itself works well. The whole lock is replaced when the mechanism is damaged or worn. A security shield protects the cylinder from the outside against snapping and extraction.

Smart lock prices

If you already have the smart lock, fitting it costs €80–150. If you would rather we supply it, the total including the device is usually €250–450, depending on the model.

It is worth checking first that it suits your door and cylinder. Some models need a specific cylinder so you can still open with a key when another key is left in on the inside.

What makes the price go up or down

Within each range, the price depends on four things:

  • The type of door and how it is shut: slammed or locked; standard, reinforced or armoured.
  • Whether parts need replacing: a broken cylinder or a worn lock adds to the job.
  • The quality of the hardware: standard, anti-bump or high-security with a key card.
  • Access: damaged frames, old locks or unusual parts take longer.
