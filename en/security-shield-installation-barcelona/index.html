Cylinder protection

The shield covers and reinforces the outside of the cylinder, one of the most common points of attack, while the key still turns easily.

Real compatibility

The measurements, lock, door and cylinder determine which shield can be fitted properly. We check them together before choosing the model.

Homes and residential buildings

We fit shields on armoured, high-security and standard doors and on shared building entrances.

Why fit a security shield

A good cylinder needs proper protection on the outside. Security shields make it harder to snap, extract or drill the cylinder. They must be fitted with the right model and to suit the door, so that everything works as intended. A good installation protects the lock without affecting how the door closes.

  • We check the lock, cylinder and door thickness.
  • Anti-snap shields and magnetic options as needed.
  • Compatible with armoured and high-security doors.
  • Shield and security cylinder assessed together.

Shield and lock change: when to do both at once

If the cylinder is old, lacks modern protection or shows wear, it may be better to renew both at the same visit. We first assess the installation and explain the options available so that you only pay for what your door needs.

Standard, armoured or magnetic shield

The choice depends on the cylinder, the lock, the thickness and material of the door, how far the cylinder sticks out and how exposed the door is. A magnetic shield adds a cover that closes over the keyhole, while other models focus on the strength of the body and its fixings. Not every shield fits every door: we measure and check everything before recommending one.

How much it costs to fit a security shield

At Cerrajería Merafex, fitting an anti-snap security shield costs €120–200, and a magnetic shield €180–260, including parts, fitting and VAT. If the cylinder also needs replacing, the two prices are added: an anti-bump cylinder with an anti-snap shield comes to roughly €260–420.
