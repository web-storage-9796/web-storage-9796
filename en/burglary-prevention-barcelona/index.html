How to improve the security of a home against theft

Effective protection comes in layers. We review the door, frame, cylinder, shield and other accesses to prioritize improvements compatible with your home. No single device is guaranteed to prevent theft, but a coherent set can increase resistance and reduce vulnerable points.

Door and frame

We check the leaf, frame, hinges, clearances and closing points. An improved lock needs a base that can properly support the stress.

Bowler hat and shield

We check the cylinder, its projection, the key control and the exterior protection to assess compatible improvements.

Other accesses

Windows, balconies, patios, garages, storage rooms and common entrances are also part of the evaluation of a home.

Safety checklist for a home

The review begins with actual access and daily use. It is not about installing everything, but rather detecting which point limits the resistance of the assembly and prioritizing the appropriate measures.

  • Condition of door, frame, hinges and anchors.
  • Type of lock, cylinder, shield and number of closing points.
  • Control of copies and action in the event of lost or stolen keys.
  • Protection of windows, patios, terraces and community accesses.
  • Lighting, visibility and closing habits when leaving.

How to reinforce a door without necessarily changing it

Depending on the condition and compatibility, a cylinder with better protections, a suitable shield, a multi-point lock or frame reinforcement can be considered. If the leaf or structure has significant limitations, it may make more sense to consider a security door. The decision must consider the whole, not just one piece.

Measurements for ground floors, attics and homes with patios

Accessible spaces require a specific inspection. Bars, opening closures, blockages or sensors can be studied depending on the window, the escape route and the community standards. In garages and shared buildings, it is also advisable to check that the doors close and that the keys or controls are removed when they are no longer authorized.

What to do if there are signs of tampering or theft

New brands, moved parts or abnormal operation do not by themselves prove a theft attempt. If you find the door open or forced, do not enter, do not touch anything, leave and call 112. Follow police instructions before documenting damage or requesting repair. Access can then be provisionally secured and the final solution evaluated.

Official security sources

For recommendations on action, consult the Mossos d'Esquadra's home security tips and the robbery and theft guide of the Generalitat of Catalonia. In an emergency call 112.
