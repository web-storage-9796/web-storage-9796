Who we are

Cerrajería Merafex, S.L. is a locksmith company based in Barcelona. Between them, our team has over 25 years of experience as locksmiths: door openings, lock and cylinder changes, security doors, and security for homes, shops and residential buildings.

We speak English, Spanish and Catalan, by phone or WhatsApp on +34 611 37 54 95 and by email at info@merafex.es.

What we do

Our main work is door opening, lock and cylinder changes, security shield installation, security door and anti-squatter door installation, locksmith work for residential buildings and advice on preventing burglaries. If you are locked out, see our emergency locksmith page.

We work with brands such as TESA ASSA ABLOY, CISA, Mul-T-Lock, ISEO, EVVA, dormakaba, DOM Security, ABUS, MCM, Yale, LINCE and JIS, and choose the model to suit the door and how you use it.

How we work

The same rules apply to every job, big or small:

  • Quotes from photos or video. Send us the door and lock on WhatsApp and we will give you a guide price, free and with no obligation.
  • Price confirmed before we start. We publish guide prices, and we confirm the final price before we touch anything.
  • Proof of address before opening. Before opening a door we ask for something showing you live there or have permission: ID with the address, a tenancy agreement or a utility bill.
  • Always an invoice with VAT. You pay when the job is done, by Visa, Mastercard, Bizum, bank transfer or cash. We never work without VAT.
  • Guarantee. Against manufacturing and installation defects: 3 months on labour and installation, and 3 years on the materials we supply.

Opening hours and areas

We work Monday to Friday, 8 am to 8 pm. There is no night-time or weekend service, so there are no night-time surcharges either. Urgent jobs are handled within those hours, subject to availability. We usually arrive within 20–30 minutes in Barcelona city, Monday to Friday, 8 am to 8 pm.
