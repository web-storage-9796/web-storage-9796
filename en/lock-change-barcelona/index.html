What we check before changing a lock

Not all doors need the same system. We check the type of lock, cylinder measurements, condition of the door, handle compatibility and the existing exterior protection. With this information we propose a replacement that maintains a correct closure and improves security. The proposal starts from your door, not from a standard solution.

  • Bowlers with property card when the model allows it.
  • Compatibility with armored, armored and conventional doors.
  • Installation of protective shields against extraction and drilling.
  • Attention for homes, premises and communities of owners.

Safety shield and bowler hat: joint protection

The cylinder is a central element of the lock, but the outer shield helps protect it against attack methods such as breaking, extraction or drilling. During the visit we check if the door supports a compatible shield and what level of protection makes sense for your case. Both pieces work best when valued as a set.

Change the cylinder or the complete lock?

If the lock works correctly and the objective is to renew the keys or improve the cylinder, it may be enough to replace the cylinder. When there is wear on the mechanism, damage after an incident or incompatibility with the desired improvement, the complete lock is evaluated. We also check if it is embedded, overlay or multi-point to avoid unnecessary replacement.
